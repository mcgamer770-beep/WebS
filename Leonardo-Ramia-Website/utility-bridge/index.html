Utility Bridge

A crossing that gives back.

A pedestrian bridge connecting the Carriageworks precinct with Redfern, conceived as both public space and local energy infrastructure. Solar harvesting and an exposed pipe network bring the distribution of heat and power into the everyday experience of crossing.

Eveleigh, SydneyInfrastructure / Public space

Individual academic project · Leonardo Ramia

Utility Bridge
01 Site & intention02 Plan & occupation03 Structure & systems04 Spatial experience
01SITE & INTENTION

Connecting across the rail corridor.

The project begins with a gap in pedestrian connections between Carriageworks and Redfern. The proposed crossing combines a public route with the collection and distribution of local energy.

02PLAN & OCCUPATION

A bridge with room to stay.

The inhabited deck combines movement with places to pause. The plans and cross section show the relationship between circulation, seating, bicycle facilities and the roof above.

03STRUCTURE & SYSTEMS

Making the infrastructure visible.

The roof, occupied deck and lower service zone are expressed as separate layers. These drawings examine how the structural assembly accommodates solar collection, storage and everyday use.

04SPATIAL EXPERIENCE

Life beneath a productive roof.

Exterior and interior perspectives explore the bridge as a public place, from the roof’s profile to the sheltered promenade and its outlook across the city.
