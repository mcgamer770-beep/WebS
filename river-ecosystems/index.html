03 / LANDSCAPE STUDIES

River Ecosystems

Reading the river at different scales.

A landscape investigation into the relationships between water, sediment, carp and algae. Catchment observations inform a series of interventions, including step pools, planted buffer pockets and a pathway that changes through dry, wet and regrowth conditions.

Glenworth Valley, NSWEcology / Landscape
